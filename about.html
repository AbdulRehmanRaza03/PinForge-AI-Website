✦ Our story

Built for affiliate marketers, by affiliate marketers

We got tired of juggling a dozen tools to run AliExpress affiliate campaigns on Pinterest — one for product sourcing, one for link building, one for scheduling, one for AI writing. So we built the single tool we always wanted.

✦ Our mission

Affiliate income you can be proud of

Most affiliate automation tools either scrape AliExpress illegally, use fake browser sessions on Pinterest, or post so aggressively that accounts get banned within weeks. We built PinForge AI to be the opposite.

Every product PinForge AI sources comes through the official AliExpress affiliate API. Every pin goes through the official Pinterest API v5 with proper OAuth2 authorization. Every affiliate link embeds your tracking ID — never ours.

We believe the best long-term affiliate strategy is one that respects both platforms' rules, serves real audiences with quality product pins, and builds sustainable USD income — not shortcuts that get your accounts banned.

✦ Our values

What we stand for

Safety first

We'd rather ship a slower, more limited feature than one that puts your Pinterest account at risk. Every safety rule is enforced server-side — not just a UI toggle.

Radical transparency

Every action PinForge AI takes on your account is logged. You can see exactly what was posted, when, to which board, and what happened — success or failure.

Quality over quantity

15 AI-optimized, properly spaced pins per day outperforms 100 spammy ones. We cap post volume not just for safety but because quality scheduling actually works better.

Accessible to everyone

Pinterest growth shouldn't require a $500/month tool or a developer on the team. Our free plan is genuinely useful — not a crippled demo.

Privacy by design

We never store your Pinterest password. We strip EXIF metadata from images before processing. We don't sell your data. Ever.

Speed of execution

From image upload to AI content to queue — under 30 seconds. We obsess over making the core workflow feel fast and effortless.

✦ The story

Why we built PinForge AI

It started with a frustrating afternoon in early 2024. I'm Abdul Rehman — I was running AliExpress affiliate campaigns on Pinterest and spending 4–5 hours a day on repetitive work. Finding trending products, building affiliate links by hand, writing pin descriptions, posting on schedule — repeat, day after day, across multiple accounts.

I looked at existing tools. Scheduling tools didn't do AliExpress sourcing. Affiliate tools didn't handle Pinterest publishing. The ones that claimed to do both either scraped AliExpress illegally or used browser automation that violated Pinterest's terms — one wrong move and an account would be banned.

So I decided to build it properly. Official AliExpress affiliate API from day one. Official Pinterest API v5 with OAuth2, not stored passwords. Real AI for content — not templates. Auto-generated affiliate links with your tracking ID embedded. Safety limits baked into the server.

PinForge AI is the tool I wish had existed — one system that takes you from AliExpress product to Pinterest commission automatically. I hope it saves you the same hours it saves me every week.

AR
Abdul Rehman — Founder
