✦ Safety first

Your accounts are protected. You stay in control.

PinForge AI runs on the official AliExpress and Pinterest APIs — no scraping, no bots, no passwords. Every safety rule is enforced server-side, and the approval queue keeps you in charge until you're ready to let the trust system take over.

  • Official AliExpress API: Products and images come from AliExpress's affiliate API, not scraping — so sourcing stays compliant.
  • Hard daily cap: Max 15 pins per Pinterest account per 24 hours. Cannot be overridden.
  • Approval queue in control: You manually approve pins until the trust system learns your style — auto-approve only when you're confident.
  • Duplicate prevention: SHA-256 content hash stops you from re-posting identical content to the same account.
